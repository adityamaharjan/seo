The Complete Guide to Sustainable Urban Gardening

Getting Started with Containers

Sustainable urban gardening lets city dwellers grow fresh food in small spaces. Container gardening is the easiest way to begin, and raised beds work well on balconies and rooftops. Choosing the right soil and compost matters for healthy plants and strong harvests throughout the growing season.

Watering and Composting

Water saving techniques such as drip irrigation and mulching reduce waste while keeping plants healthy. Composting kitchen scraps creates rich soil and closes the loop on household waste, making your urban garden more sustainable every single year you maintain it carefully and thoughtfully.

Choosing Plants

Herbs, leafy greens, tomatoes, and peppers thrive in containers. Pick varieties suited to your light conditions and local climate for the best results from your sustainable urban garden.
